FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need DaVinci Resolve Studio? +

No. The system works with both the Free and Studio versions of DaVinci Resolve. The LUTs also work in Premiere, Final Cut or any NLE that reads .cube files.

Does it work with my camera? +

Yes. The PowerGrade is camera-agnostic, and premade versions for Sony, Canon, Fuji and DJI are included.

Do I need to shoot in Log? +

Ideally yes — Log gives you the most flexibility. But the tutorials also cover how to adapt the workflow to Rec.709 footage.

How do I receive the files? +

Instant download via Lemon Squeezy right after purchase, with free updates for this version.
