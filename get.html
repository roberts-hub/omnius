Before you buy.

Which edition should I get? +

If you edit in DaVinci Resolve, get the Full System: you get the PowerGrade node tree, the LUTs and the 50-minute masterclass. If you edit in Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro or CapCut, the LUT Pack gives you the SPECTRE look as .cube files.

Do I need DaVinci Resolve Studio? +

No. The PowerGrade works in DaVinci Resolve Free and Studio. The LUTs work in any editor that supports .cube files.

What cameras does it work with? +

SPECTRE works with any camera. It includes versions made specifically for Sony S-Log3, DJI D-Log, Canon C-Log and RED, plus a Rec.709 version for footage shot without a Log profile. If you shoot on another camera, the PowerGrade can be adapted to your footage, and the masterclass shows you how.

How do I get my files? +

Right after paying you get an email to create your SPECTRE account. Log in at spectrecolor.com/access with your email and password: your downloads, the guides and the tutorials are there, and you keep access to future updates.

Can I use it for client work? +

Yes. You can use SPECTRE on unlimited personal and commercial projects, including client work. The license is for one person.
